Metacognitive self-referential agent

The agent that doubts its own body

One step, in order

  1. Sense resources, damage and the time, and add the current doubt to make the state.
  2. Score every action: its value, minus β times the self-model's uncertainty and risk of dying.
  3. Act, and see what the body did.
  4. Compare that with what the self-model predicted.
  5. Fold the surprise into doubt, ready for the next state.
  6. Learn: the value network and the self-model both train on the new experience.

What the experiments found

Learning to survive. Hard difficulty, no hidden faults. 400 training episodes, then 200 test episodes; mean of 3 training runs.

Fear of death is the part that makes survival reliable. It also earns more reward, because the agent stops losing whole episodes.

Surviving a hidden fault. Survival in episodes where a hidden fault struck; hard difficulty. 500 training episodes, then 300 test episodes; mean of 3 training runs. "Caught it" is how often doubt crossed the alarm level within 15 steps of a severe fault.
