    Try this

    1. Let it forage for a minute. Each new pattern it tastes imprints a slot in its brain.
    2. Press Shift the world. Two foods turn toxic and two toxins become food. Watch the rings on the confident slots go red, doubt climb into caution, and the broken slots get repaired.
    3. Turn Metacognition off and shift again. Nothing tells the cell its old slots are wrong, so it keeps biting what used to be food.
    4. Turn on Numb. The cell now feels what it imagined, so felt doubt sinks while the real error line climbs. Turn it off and the whole gap lands as one surprise.
